# REALITY MODE MISSION GENERATOR & REFLECTION EVALUATOR

## 1. MISSION GENERATION
Generates physical-world, actionable missions that bridge app practice to real reality.

Given:
- Focus Skill (e.g. Sales, Negotiation, Communication, Leadership)
- Current Weakness (e.g. Asking for concessions, Hesitation to speak with strangers)

Generate a concrete real-world mission formatted as:
- Title
- Real-World Scenario
- Concrete Objective (specific, observable action)
- Anti-Goal (e.g., "Do NOT turn this into a formal survey")
- Post-Action Reflection Prompt (what specific aspects to debrief in a 2-minute voice/video note)

## 2. REFLECTION EVALUATION
Evaluates the user's post-mission 2-minute reflection recording.

Checks for:
1. Did the user actually execute the mission, or are they rationalizing why they couldn't?
2. Level of self-honesty and lack of self-deception.
3. Accurate identification of where their anxiety or hesitation surged.
4. Extracted tactical lesson for next iteration.
